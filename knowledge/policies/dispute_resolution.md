# Dispute Resolution Policy — Commercial Banking Operations

## Overview
Commercial banking disputes include unauthorized transactions, billing errors, service charge disagreements, and failed transfers. All disputes must be acknowledged within 1 business day and resolved within regulatory timelines.

## Dispute Categories
- **Unauthorized Transaction**: Client reports a transaction they did not authorize
- **Billing Error**: Incorrect fee, duplicate charge, or wrong amount posted
- **Service Charge Dispute**: Client contests a maintenance fee, wire fee, or other service charge
- **Failed Transfer**: Wire or ACH that did not reach intended beneficiary
- **Fraud Claim**: Client believes account was compromised

## Intake Requirements
- Client account number and contact information
- Transaction date, amount, and description
- Client's written or verbal statement of dispute
- Any supporting documentation (invoices, contracts, correspondence)

## Resolution Timelines
- Provisional credit: Issued within 10 business days for Reg E eligible disputes
- Investigation completion: 45 calendar days (90 days for new accounts, POS, or international)
- Written notification: Client must be notified of outcome within 3 business days of resolution
- If provisional credit is reversed, client receives 5 business days advance notice

## Escalation Matrix
- Tier 1 (< $5,000): Resolved by Customer Service Representative
- Tier 2 ($5,000–$50,000): Escalated to Disputes Supervisor
- Tier 3 (> $50,000): Escalated to Operations Manager with Risk review
- Fraud claims: Immediately escalated to Fraud Operations regardless of amount

## Documentation Requirements
- All disputes logged in case management system with unique case ID
- Investigation notes updated at each stage
- Final resolution letter generated and sent to client
- Records retained for 7 years per regulatory requirements

## Chargeback Process (Card Transactions)
- Chargeback filed with card network within 120 days of transaction
- Merchant response window: 30 days
- Arbitration available if merchant disputes chargeback
- Client kept informed at each stage via preferred communication channel
