# Account Maintenance Policy — Commercial Banking Operations

## Overview
Account maintenance encompasses all non-transactional changes to commercial banking accounts including signer updates, address changes, account closures, and product modifications.

## Authorized Signer Changes
- Addition: Requires board resolution or partnership agreement amendment
- Removal: Requires written request from remaining authorized signer(s)
- Replacement: Both addition and removal documentation required
- All signer changes require identity verification (government-issued ID)
- Changes take effect next business day after processing

## Address and Contact Updates
- Business address change: Requires authorized signer request plus supporting documentation (lease, utility bill, or filing)
- Mailing address: Authorized signer verbal or written request
- Phone/email: Authorized signer verbal request with identity verification
- All changes logged with timestamp and agent ID

## Account Closure
- Written request required from authorized signer
- Outstanding transactions must clear before closure
- Remaining balance disbursed via official bank check or wire
- Minimum 5 business day processing period
- Dormant accounts (no activity 12+ months): Subject to state unclaimed property procedures
- Accounts with outstanding loans or credit facilities cannot be closed without payoff

## Product Changes
- Upgrade/downgrade: Effective at next statement cycle
- Adding services (lockbox, sweep, ZBA): Requires service agreement
- Removing services: Written request with 30-day notice
- Fee schedule changes communicated 30 days in advance

## Compliance Requirements
- All maintenance requests verified against authorized signer list
- Beneficial ownership updates required for material changes in ownership structure
- CIP re-verification required for signer additions
- SAR filing considered for unusual maintenance patterns (rapid signer changes, frequent address updates)

## Documentation and Retention
- All maintenance requests documented in account notes
- Supporting documents scanned and attached to account record
- Audit trail maintained for all changes
- Records retained for life of account plus 7 years
