# Wire Transfer Policy — Commercial Banking Operations

## Overview
All domestic and international wire transfers must comply with internal controls, BSA/AML requirements, and OFAC screening before execution.

## Authorization Thresholds
- Transfers under $10,000: Single authorized signer required
- Transfers $10,000–$100,000: Dual authorization required
- Transfers over $100,000: Dual authorization plus manager approval
- International transfers: Always require dual authorization regardless of amount

## Processing Windows
- Domestic wires: Submitted before 4:00 PM ET for same-day processing
- International wires: Submitted before 2:00 PM ET for same-day initiation
- Late submissions are queued for next business day

## Required Information
- Originator account number and name
- Beneficiary name, account number, and bank routing/SWIFT code
- Transfer amount and currency
- Purpose of payment (required for international transfers)
- Reference number if applicable

## Compliance Checks
- All wire transfers are screened against OFAC Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list
- Transfers flagged by AML monitoring require Compliance review before release
- Repeat high-value transfers to new beneficiaries trigger enhanced due diligence
- Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs) filed for cash-related transactions over $10,000

## Error Resolution
- Same-day recall requests must be submitted within 30 minutes of execution
- Recall success depends on beneficiary bank cooperation
- Misdirected wires require immediate escalation to Wire Operations Manager
- All recall attempts are logged in the case management system

## Cutoff Exceptions
- Emergency wires after cutoff require VP-level approval
- Holiday schedule wires must be pre-scheduled 2 business days in advance
